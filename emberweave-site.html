About the game

Emberweave Heroes is a 3D hero-battler set in a scorched, ember-lit world. Summon a roster of mythic heroes — tanks, mages, assassins and more — level and ascend them, and send your team through story campaigns, city raids, and a ranked arena. Battles play out in real time with signature abilities and ultimates, while everything you earn syncs to your account so you can pick up on any device.
