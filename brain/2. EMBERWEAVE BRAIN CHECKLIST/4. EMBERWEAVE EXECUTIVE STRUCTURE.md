Operational Rules:

  - Departments own their assigned directories.
  - No department may overwrite another department's files.
  - Cross-department changes require interface documentation.
  - All major decisions must be documented in docs/.
  - Claude has final authority on implementation decisions.
  - ChatGPT has final authority on architectural decisions.
  - Grok provides research recommendations.
  - Kimi serves as a specialist advisor when available.

Mission Statement:
  Emberweave is a network of specialized intelligences working
  together as a unified intelligence operating system. Each
  department maintains ownership of its domain while collaborating
  through documented interfaces, shared memory, and common goals.
