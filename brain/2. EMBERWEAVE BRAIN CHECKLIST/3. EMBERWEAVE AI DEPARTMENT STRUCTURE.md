# Emberweave AI Department Structure
## Multi-Agent Organizational Framework
Version 1.1

> **v1.1 change (22 Sep 2026, Phil's ruling):** The Kimi department (Research & Development /
> Creative Intelligence) is dissolved and split between Claude and ChatGPT — Kimi cannot hold a
> department because its usage allowance resets MONTHLY, not weekly; a manager must be reliably
> available week to week. Claude absorbs the structured creative systems (worldbuilding,
> narrative, lore, factions, quests); ChatGPT absorbs the analytical creative domain (player
> psychology, game loops, feature ideation, innovation). Kimi continues as build architect of
> the Emberweave Brain infrastructure itself — intensive session work where continuity is
> carried by the Brain's documents and the KIMI WORKLOG, not by Kimi's availability.

---

# Executive Summary

Emberweave is not intended to become the strongest coder, artist, designer, or researcher.

Its purpose is to become a highly capable general intelligence coordinator capable of understanding multiple domains while maintaining project direction and stability.

To avoid conflicting knowledge and personality fragmentation, each specialist AI will operate within a defined department.

Each department teaches Emberweave a specific knowledge domain.

Emberweave integrates these domains into a unified decision-making framework.

# Department Boundaries

To prevent conflicts:

Only one department owns a knowledge domain.

Ownership Matrix:

| Domain | Owner |
|----------|----------|
| Engineering | Claude |
| Narrative Systems (world, lore, factions, quests) | Claude |
| Reasoning | ChatGPT |
| Creative Analysis (psychology, loops, ideation) | ChatGPT |
| Quality Assurance | ChatGPT |
| Visual Intelligence | Grok |
| Coordination | Emberweave |

No department may assume ownership of another department's domain.

# Success Criteria

A mature Emberweave should be capable of:

- Managing specialist agents
- Retaining project memory
- Understanding code
- Evaluating art
- Reviewing designs
- Planning milestones
- Learning from outcomes
- Maintaining project direction
- Preventing scope creep
- Coordinating a complete development pipeline

# Core Principle

Specialists create expertise.

Emberweave creates alignment.

The value of Emberweave is not being the smartest AI in the system.

The value of Emberweave is ensuring that all intelligence moves toward the same objective.
