# 13 · THE GAUNTLET SPLIT — **Phil's pick, his rules.** Spec, 16 Sep 2026.

**Implementation decision from that isolation rule:** A whole-field or unusually large area ability still affects every valid unit **inside its caster's lane** in Gauntlet, including Magmourn's Ignite All when he is the middle-road x-6 boss. It never reaches another skirmish; the same ability remains unchanged in ordinary Campaign. Filter by combat context before resolving an area effect, not by assuming a measured gap will always exceed its radius. The three per-lane combat updates share one rendered frame: projectiles, particles, floating text and FX lifetime updates run once after the lane steps, not three times.

**Proportional spell footprint:** With Gauntlet units and ground spell visuals at 0.40 of ordinary scale, finite damaging/healing/CC radii are scaled proportionally in Gauntlet only, so a spell covers roughly the same fraction of a lane formation as its Campaign version. Keep the visible effect and hit footprint aligned. Explicit whole-lane effects remain whole lane through context filtering, and Campaign numbers do not change. Painted road bands are movement limits; knockback, charge, summons and collisions cannot force a unit into divider scenery. Lane transfers follow the painted firebreak paths rather than a diagonal through scenery.

**Three lanes. Up to ten of your own heroes. You assign every one to a lane, commit, and the lanes run
at the same time** — your heroes walk forward killing waves that come at them, **five waves per lane**.
