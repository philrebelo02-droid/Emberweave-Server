Facebook data deletion

If you sign in to Emberweave Heroes with Facebook, we keep only your Facebook user ID for this app, and the name and email address Facebook shares with us, so we can sign you back in to the same account. We do not post to Facebook or read your friends, photos or messages.

Deletion request received? If you arrived here from Facebook after removing the app, your request has been handled: the Facebook ID, name and email linked to your Emberweave Heroes account have been removed from our servers. Keep the confirmation code Facebook showed you if you contact us about it.

Remove only the Facebook link

  1. Open the game and go to Settings > Account.
  2. Select Unlink Facebook and enter the code we email to your verified recovery address. (If Facebook is your only way to sign in, set a password first.)

Or remove Emberweave Heroes from Facebook > Settings & privacy > Settings > Apps and websites and choose to send a deletion request. We then remove the Facebook data automatically.

Delete your whole game account

  1. In the game: Settings > Account > Delete account.
  2. Without the game: use the request form on our Privacy Policy page.
  3. Or email emberweaveheroes@gmail.com from the address linked to your account, with your username, and ask us to delete it.

Account deletion has a 7-day grace period, after which the account and its game data are wiped, as described in the Privacy Policy.
