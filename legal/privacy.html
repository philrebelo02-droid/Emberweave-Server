We use this information to provide and maintain the Game, authenticate accounts, synchronize saves, process entitlements, communicate about support or service changes, moderate community features, maintain security and fair play, diagnose and improve the service, and comply with legal obligations.

Where applicable data-protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on providing the service you request, our legitimate interests in operating and protecting the Game, compliance with legal obligations, or consent where it is required. We do not treat acceptance of the Terms as consent to unrelated optional data processing. You can contact us about applicable choices and rights.

3. Sharing and visibility

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as needed for the purposes described here:

  • With other players when you use a feature that displays your name, game activity or contributions to them.
  • With hosting, storage, backup, communications and other service providers that help us operate and support the Game, for those service purposes.
  • With Google Play for purchase validation, billing-related functions and refunds. Google also processes information under Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • With authorities or other relevant parties when required by law, to respond to a valid legal request, or where necessary to protect people, investigate fraud or defend legal rights.

We limit access to the information needed for the relevant purpose. If a business transfer affects personal information, applicable privacy obligations continue and we will provide any notice required by law.

4. Storage and security

Account information, game saves, chat, purchase records and service logs are stored in systems used to operate the Game. We also maintain backups for recovery. Information is processed on our systems and by the service providers described above; their location may differ from the country where you live. Where a transfer requires legal safeguards, we use the safeguards required by applicable law.

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, including access controls and password hashing. No online service or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Tell us promptly if you suspect unauthorized access to your account.

5. Retention and backups

We retain account information and game saves while your account is active so we can provide the Game. Chat and support information are kept as needed to provide those features, respond to reports and support requests, and meet the limited purposes described in this Policy.

Routine technical, connection, IP and diagnostic logs are retained for 90 days. Information genuinely needed for an unresolved security or fraud investigation, a legal claim or a statutory recordkeeping obligation may be retained separately for as long as that specific purpose requires. We restrict that retained information to the relevant purpose rather than continuing normal account use.

When your account is deleted, we remove its associated personal information from active databases after the seven-day grace period described below, subject to those limited retention needs. Purchase or refund records that must be kept for tax, accounting, fraud prevention or legal obligations may outlast the account. Information that has been irreversibly anonymized so it no longer identifies you may also remain.

Backups age out on their existing backup rotation schedule. They are not all erased immediately when an account is deleted. Backup copies are kept for recovery and are not used to reactivate a deleted account for ordinary gameplay. If recovery ever requires restoring an older backup, we reapply completed deletions to the restored data before it goes back into use. Contact us if you need information about the backup schedule applicable to your request.

6. Delete your account and associated data

You can request account deletion in either of these ways:

  1. In the Game: open Settings and select Delete account in the Cloud Account panel. Type DELETE and your password to confirm.
  2. On the web, without reinstalling the Game: use the deletion request form below this section with your username and password. The request starts the same seven-day grace period.
  3. By email, if you cannot use your password: email emberweaveheroes@gmail.com with the subject Emberweave Heroes account deletion. Include your username or account ID and a way for us to reply. Do not send your password or full payment details. We may ask for information reasonably needed to verify that the account is yours; if you cannot use the account’s sign-in method, explain that in your request so we can help you verify ownership another way.

After a verified request, we lock and hide your account and begin a seven-day grace period: the account is signed out on every device, cannot be played, and its castle is removed from the world map. You can cancel deletion by signing in within those seven days; signing in cancels the deletion and unlocks the account. If you do not cancel, our servers wipe the account and its associated personal data from our active databases when the grace period ends, including the username, password hash, recovery email, profile, game saves, city and guild membership, except for the specific legal, security or fraud-prevention records explained above. This is permanent deletion, rather than an indefinite account freeze.

Messages you posted in shared chats (world, region and guild chat) that other players read stay in those chats, but they are unlinked from your account and shown as from "Deleted player". The same applies to records other players depend on, such as guild war and battle records. A guest profile that has no username and password cannot be signed back into, so it is deleted at once, without a grace period.

Backups age out on their existing schedule as described in section 5. Messages or screenshots that other players have independently copied outside our systems are outside our control. Deleting the app alone does not delete your account or its data. A Game-account deletion does not delete your Google account, Google’s own records or an independently managed store subscription. If you have a Google Play subscription, manage its cancellation in Google Play as well.

You may also email us about deletion of particular personal information without closing the account. We will explain if that information is needed to provide the requested account feature or must be retained for a specific lawful reason.

Web deletion request

Enter the username and password of the account to delete. The account is locked at once and wiped after seven days unless you sign in before then.
